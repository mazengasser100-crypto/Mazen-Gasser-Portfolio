LIVECART INTERACTIVE WEBSITE — FRONT-END DEMO

1. Unzip Livecart-Interactive-Website.zip
2. Open the livecart folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
4. Keep the assets folder, style.css and script.js alongside index.html.

Includes: responsive home, about, contact, login/signup demo, live/video/store tabs,
filters, product detail, size/color/quantity, cart, checkout and confirmation.

NOTE: This is a portfolio demonstration, not a production shopping platform.
Account information and cart are local to your browser; payments are NOT charged.
Google login, real livestreams, inventory, payment processing and message delivery
require backend and third-party service integration.

The images are cropped from the design screenshots you supplied.
